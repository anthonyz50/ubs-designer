# UBS Capitalisation and Spelling

> Source: UBS Frontify Portal, Standard Basics > Design Basics & Brand > Capitalisation and Spelling

## Capitalisation

**Sentence case** is the standard, except for names of things.

Examples:
- "Investing in alternative assets" — a headline, sentence case ✓
- "Brand Design Platform" — a name, each word capitalised ✓
- "UBS Application Design Style Guide" — a name, each word capitalised ✓

Follow appropriate rules for each language (e.g. capitalisation of nouns in German).

## Spelling

UBS's standard is to use **American English** spelling (e.g. "color" not "colour").

**Exceptions:**
- Applications used only in British-spelling countries (UK, Australia, etc.) may use British English
- Where feasible, spelling can change based on client domicile

**Sentence forms:** UBS does not mandate US sentence forms. Use whichever is appropriate for the audience.
- US: "I wrote the President Tuesday" (valid American)
- UK: "I wrote to the President on Tuesday" (British English)
