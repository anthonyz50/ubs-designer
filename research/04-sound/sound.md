# Sound

> Listen up to our human, warm, powerful and digital savvy sound system

## Top Takeaways

1. Sound is a powerful connector that evokes emotion and makes our brand memorable.
2. Always use our sound logo or adaptation and select music that fits with its instrumentation, tonality and key.
3. Never alter the sound logo or adaptation. Always provide needed space between music and sound logo.

---

## Sound Principles

### Intended Emotional Response & Characteristics

| Emotion | Sound Is | Do | Don't |
|---------|----------|-----|-------|
| Comfortable & valued | **Warm & human** | Use sounds that feel round, natural, and human. Keep it simple and uncluttered. | No harsh, metallic, or very dark sounds that feel distant or cold. |
| Confident, safe, strong, trust, stability | **Powerful** | Use steady rhythms, clear melodies, sounds that feel solid, confident and grounded. | Not overly loud, dramatic, or chaotic. No fragile or hectic sounds. |
| Forward-thinking, innovative | **Digital savvy** | Clear and spacious, evolving subtly to spark curiosity and progress. | No rigid formulas or clichés (Sci-Fi or corporate orchestral) or static/uninspired sounds. |

## Sound Logo

### Principal Sound Logo
- **Key:** E major (relative minor: C# minor)
- **Suitable instruments:** synth, mallets, harp, plucked sounds
- **Suitable styles:** minimal, electronic, downtempo
- **Suitable energy:** lively, delicate, dynamic

**The principal sound logo should always be the first choice.**

### Sound Logo Adaptations

## Application Standards

### Integrity
- Use sound logos exactly as provided. They are finalised assets.
- **Don't:** modify in any way (shortening, extending, transposing, speeding up, slowing down, or any audio processing like reverb, distortion, filtering).

### Position
- Introduce the sound logo between **0 and 350 ms** after any music, background sound, or voice-over
- Let previous audio fade out naturally, avoiding abrupt cuts
- **Don't:** place directly after other audio without spacing or create harsh transitions

### Loudness
- All sound logos are mastered to maximum peak and full dynamic range (approx. **-12 LUFS**)
- Lower volume only if needed for overall mix
- **Don't:** attempt to increase loudness (may cause clipping/distortion)

### File Specifications
- Use only official UBS audio files from the download section
- **Don't:** convert to other formats or re-encode lossy formats
