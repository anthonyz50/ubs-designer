# Accessibility

> Accessibility is an opportunity. With everything we create, we want to reach our audience.

We lay importance on that everybody, despite disabilities, can access, see and read what we share, and interact with UBS.

---

## Standards

### WCAG Compliance
- **UBS adheres to WCAG 2.2, level AA**
- Based on "universal design" principles
- Developed by W3C (World Wide Web Consortium)
- Three conformance levels: A, AA, AAA

### Best Practice: WCAG for Print
WCAG applies not only to websites and apps, but also to documents (PDFs from Office/Adobe) and printed products (brochures, flyers, magazines).

**Why apply WCAG to print?**
- Guidelines take into account all types of disabilities
- Printed products often published later as PDFs (must comply)
- Important for people with slight impairments (age, situational limitations)

## Criteria for Printed Materials

### Layout and Text

#### Design Reminders
- All content must work for PDF reflow and enlargement up to **400%**
- Large/complex infographics may not display properly on mobile
- Prepare content in pieces for mobile display

#### Clear Structure
- Content must fit into logical heading structure
- Give each content block a title

#### Sufficient Type Size
- **10.5 pt (14 CSS px):** Usually the minimum
- **12 pt (16 CSS px):** Easy-to-read standard, with line height of 17 pt (22 CSS px)

#### Space for Text
- Avoid fixed heights for text-containing elements
- Leave room for text to expand vertically

#### Good Contrast
- **Text:** Background colour contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1**
- **Large text (over 18 pt / 25 CSS px):** Contrast ratio at least **3:1**
- **Icons and graphic objects:** Contrast ratio at least **3:1** to background or adjacent colours

#### Colour Contrast Tools
- Desktop app: [Colour Contrast Analyser](https://developer.paciellogroup.com/resources/contrastanalyser)
- Online: [contrastchecker.com](https://contrastchecker.com)

### Pictures and Infographics

- **Avoid text in images.** Where unavoidable, ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast.
- **Colour contrast in charts:** At least 3:1 foreground to background/neighbouring colour, or use clear separator/space of at least 2 CSS px
- **Don't rely on colour alone.** Colourblind users need additional design features.
- **Alternative text:** Add short description of visual meaning in alt-text
- **Provide alternatives for important visual content:** Title, concise description, core statement summary, complete data in text form

## Success Criteria for Barrier-Free PDFs

Accessibility begins with structure in the source document before converting to PDF.

### Checklist

1. **Document Properties**
   - Document title (in properties: description > title)
   - Document language definition (in properties: advanced > reading options)

2. **Tags Set Inside Document**
   - Headings and heading structure
   - Alternative text for images
   - Table headings
   - Form field labels

3. **Tag Optimisation**
   - Use Acrobat Pro > Tools > Accessibility > Full Check

### SEO Benefit
Accessible PDFs are much better recognised and indexed by search engines. Optimisation has big impact on page ranking and hit accuracy.
