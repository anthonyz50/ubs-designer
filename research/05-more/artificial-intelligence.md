# Artificial Intelligence (AI)

> Understand the practical use of AI from a brand perspective

**Note:** AI technology is advancing quickly, and legal regulations might change. This information will be updated on an ongoing basis.

## Top Takeaways

1. We are a **warm and human brand**. We apply AI purposefully, using it only where it clearly improves quality, insight and efficiency, and always with human oversight and audience understanding. Every AI-supported output must stay aligned with our core beliefs.
2. We **never use AI to create misleading, deceptive, manipulative content** or AI-generated content that could distort people, events, or reality.
3. Follow the do-and-don't for each asset type and use approved tools in line with UBS brand, IP and policies. Apply labels as required and preserve vendor-managed metadata.

---

## Core Beliefs

### 1. AI as an Enabler of Human Potential
Use AI to amplify human skills and creativity. AI serves as a complementary tool, empowering teams to dedicate more time to high-impact work.

### 2. Build on Human Craft for Genuine Emotions
Real emotions come from real people. Move hearts through human-created content. Tell authentic stories that resonate with audiences.

### 3. AI with Human Ownership
Maintain full accountability for AI-driven results. Human oversight guides every output.

### 4. Committed to Ethical and Responsible Use
Ensure AI meets highest ethical standards. Stand by principles of transparency and accountability.

### 5. Continuous Learning for a Changing Future
Foster a culture of curiosity, embracing rapid evolution of AI technologies.

## AI and Intellectual Property (IP)

- AI-generated content typically lacks copyright protection
- Secure appropriate consents/licences for protected IP
- **Advise against using wholly AI generated images/videos** in branding, marketing and communication
- **Do not use UBS image database images** (including Getty Images) for transformative AI, machine learning, or image generation purposes
- **Exception:** Generative fill/colour/expand capabilities in Photoshop can be used on UBS/Getty Royalty-free images

### UBS Policies
- UBS brand policy (1-P-003402)
- Intellectual Property Policy (1-P-000132)
- Guidelines (1-G-002865)

## AI Labelling

### Implicit Labels
- Implemented by software vendor automatically (Adobe, Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI, etc.)
- Do not actively remove vendor-managed metadata
- Avoid screenshots, re-saving without metadata, online converters, or copy-paste that removes labels

### Explicit Labels
**Required when:**
- Content is partially or completely AI-generated or its main subject is AI-generated
- Used in high-trust or sensitive context where it could be misleading
- Always required for deepfakes

**Not required when:**
- AI performs assistive function for standard editing
- Does not substantially alter input data or semantics
- Content shared only internally (except deepfakes)

### Label Types
- **Partially AI-Generated:** Hand-and-stars icon + "edited using AI"
- **Completely AI-Generated:** Stars icon + "generated using AI"

### Label Sentence Template
"This [image / illustration / ...] has been [edited / generated] using AI"

### Label Placement
1. **Primary:** Bottom left corner (default)
2. **Secondary:** Bottom right
3. **Last option:** Top right
- **Never** place in same corner as UBS logo

## AI Imagery

### Permitted Uses
- **Ideation:** AI visuals for internal mood boards, preliminary concepts, initial mockups
- **Production:** Abstract graphics/patterns, generic objects, imaginary/futuristic objects, fictive/abstract avatars
- **Post-production:** Basic enhancements (colour correction, sharpening), background removal/replacement, optimisation for web/social

### Don't (Imagery)
- No creating/altering unique identifiable objects (mountains, identifiable buildings)
- No using pre-designed avatars from video generation platforms
- No creating/altering sensitive or culturally significant images
- No replicating content without rights
- No creating core brand identity visuals (logos, icons)
- No photo-realistic images of humans, especially faces
- No images not on brand
- No images that distort truth
- No low-quality visuals with generative artefacts

## AI Video

### Permitted Uses
- Ideation: Internal mood boards, preliminary concepts
- Production: Abstract footage/patterns, unique visual patterns, futuristic objects, fictive avatars
- Post-production: Basic enhancements, background removal, optimisation

### Don't (Video)
- Same restrictions as imagery
- No creating/altering unique identifiable subjects
- No photo-realistic videos of identifiable humans
- No core brand identity visuals (logo outro, motion principles)

## AI Avatars

### Core Characteristics
- Human-like appearance
- Natural voice and tone
- Visual and verbal customisation
- Multiple languages and accents
- Can be based on real people or digital characters

### Brand Alignment
Must reflect: brand personality, visual identity, tone of voice

### Key Principles
- **Respect audience:** Consider cultural, emotional, contextual factors
- **Prioritise authenticity:** For trust/empathy messages, use real people
- **Purpose-driven use:** Enhance clarity, scalability, accessibility
- **Transparency:** Always disclose when AI-generated

### Appropriate Uses
- Internal onboarding and training
- Product walkthroughs/explainers
- Customer service FAQs
- Brand marketing and storytelling
- Financial literacy/educational content

### Avoid For
- Crisis/reputational risk communication
- Legal, HR, disciplinary content
- Executive/leadership announcements
- Situations requiring emotional empathy
