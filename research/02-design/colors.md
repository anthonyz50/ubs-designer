# Colors

> Color our world with a unique look and feel that serves a clear function

## Top Takeaways

1. **White** is our content stage, and dominates the other colours. We write in **Black** and use **UBS Red** to emphasise content.
2. With our **Gray** and **Bordeaux** tones, we support the warm and human tonality. And the **Bronze** tones help to set accents.
3. Our two **Pastel** shades are used for backgrounds. In combination with the Gray and Bordeaux tones, they complete the secondary colours.

## Colour Contrast and Accessibility

It is important that all UBS materials, from presentations to brochures, can be clearly read also by individuals affected by visual impairment.

### Ensure readability through:
- High contrast for typography
- Alternating light and dark tones in a sequence
- Clear diagrams with a key/legend in close proximity to relevant content
- White dividing lines in UBS diagrams as breathing space between data segments

### Text on Colour
- In general, UBS Red is the highlight colour for text on White
- When setting highlights on Pastel backgrounds, always opt for Bordeaux I

### Colour Ratio
In general, use the colours in the defined ratio. Depending on the application and context, not all colours are always used and the ratio may vary.

### Usage Examples:
- **Accent:** Use specific colours for their intended function (e.g. Bronze I for accents)
- **Standard:** Use colours carefully, not combining all available colours
- **Variation:** Ensure variety within an application, balanced overall impression
- **Context:** Consider the type of application when designing
- **Harmony:** Derive secondary colour choices based on image backgrounds
- **Big picture:** Check platform and sequence for overall impression

## Metallic

Where material, use only metals with a silver sheen, e.g. polished or brushed aluminium, stainless steel. Where printed, simulate the look of stainless steel. Use silver foil stamping or Pantone metallic ink.

### Metallic Silver
- **HEX:** #BEBEBE
- **RGB:** 190, 190, 190
- **PMS:** Silver C, 877
- **PMS-CP:** Silver 10077 C

### Don't
- Do not apply any other metallic colours
- No gold, copper, brass, etc.
