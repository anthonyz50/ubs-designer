# Premises & Signage

> Giving our signs power of presence, from the streets to the skylines

## Top Takeaways

1. At the beginning of a concept, determine local signage needs.
2. Divide signage into three concept types: long distance, entrance area, and in-house.
3. Use **bold logo** for signage viewed up to 15 metres or less; **extra bold** from 15 metres or more.

---

## Signage Categories

### Long-Distance Signage
Signals the brand and guides visitors from far away.

### Entrance-Area Signage
Connects inside and outside, allowing smooth transition. Reconfirms UBS location and provides further information.

### In-House Signage
Guides visitors directly and clearly within the building. Shows current location and gives directions.

---

## Logo Weights for Signage

### Bold
For signage viewed **up to 15 metres** or less.

### Extra Bold
For signage viewed **from 15 metres** or more.
- Uses half the height of the Key Symbol to determine clear space.

### Key Symbol (Standalone)
- Only in environments already branded with UBS
- Clear space: one-third of Key Symbol height

---

## Typography for Signage
- **Frutiger Roman** for all text (never Frutiger Italic)
- Align with left edge of Key Symbol
- Adapt type size to reading distance and signage concept
- Line spacing: **120%-140%** of type size (baseline to baseline)
- Directional arrows: pointer at least cap height of type size

---

## Colours and Materials
- High-grade materials and corporate colours
- Modern and simple construction
- Clear layout and typography
- Corporate colours only

---

## Long-Distance Sign Types

### Illuminated Individual Letters
- Primary building identification from long distance
- For prestigious buildings and prominent locations
- **Light facade:** Key Symbol appears black by day, white by night
- **Dark facade:** Key Symbol white acrylic glass with translucent white foil
- From 3rd floor up: use extra bold logo
- Brand name always appears in red

### Facade Band
- Black horizontal band with vertical markers at both ends
- Logo marks main entrance
- Continues across all visible/accessible sides

### Illuminated Boxes
- Mounted on facade band or directly on facade
- Extended visibility from various angles
- Self-service versions indicate ATM zones (24/7)

### Freestanding Illuminated Boxes
- Where facade band not permitted (architecture, regulations, landmark protection)

### Illuminated Pylons (Vertical/Horizontal)
- Orientation for pedestrians and vehicles near UBS locations

---

## Entrance-Area Signage

### Sign Types
- Square sign (wall-mounted)
- Two square signs (positioned vertically)
- Vertical sign
- Rectangular sign
- Building directory sign
- Glass door lettering
- Key Symbol and logo (individual letters) in reception area

---

## In-House Signage

- **Logo never appears on in-house signs**
- Location sign
- Information sign
- Room sign

---

## Standard Grid for Signage

Six aspects:
1. Sign format
2. Logo size
3. Logo position
4. Margins
5. Clear space around logo
6. Text size and direction
