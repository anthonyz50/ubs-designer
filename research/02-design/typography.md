# Typography

> Face a typeface with clearly defined proportions for clarity and readability

## Top Takeaways

1. Our corporate typeface is **Frutiger**. Whenever possible we use the font style **Frutiger Light**. If this is not possible due to technical reasons, **Arial** replaces Frutiger.
2. We generally write our font in **black on a white background**. On dark backgrounds we use a **white font**.
3. For certain defined highlights we use **UBS red** as the font colour. A **bold** font style can also be used, where appropriate. Review the messaging chapter for more info on message highlighting with colour.

## Hierarchy Ratios

We distinguish between keyline and infoline. The font size of the keyline is at least **twice the size** of the font size of the infoline.

## Quotes

Quote marks vary by language:
- **English**: Standard English quotation marks
- **German (Austria and Germany)**: German-style quotation marks
- **Swiss, French, Italian, Arabic, Portuguese and Spanish**: Guillemets
- **Cyrillic (Russian)**: Russian quotation marks

## Font Licensing and Download

UBS's corporate typeface is licensed to UBS and its partners with specific agreements in place for each character set.

### Latin
Frutiger typeface is licensed through **Monotype** for both internal users and external partners across:
- Desktop
- Websites
- Applications
- Mobile apps
- Servers
- Digital impressions (digital marketing communications)

UBS has the right to sublicense the Latin character set to external partners for UBS projects only.

**Not included:** Commercial electronic document license (documents designed for commercialisation).

### Chinese
DF Hei Std (Simplified Chinese) W3 + W5 and DF Hei HK (Traditional Chinese) W3 + W5 are licensed only to internal users for:
- Printed material
- Multimedia advertisement
- Video products
- Websites
- Portable electronic files

External partners need to purchase a desktop license.

### Japanese
Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN W2 + W4 + W6 are licensed to 100 internal users only for:
- Printed material
- Multimedia advertisement
- Video products
- Portable electronic files

## Don't

- No Unapproved colour
- No UBS red for numbers
- No Opacity
- No Small caps
- No Encoding or prime mark as quotation mark
- No Different font sizes in one sentence
- No Stairs
- No Justification
- No Right aligned
- No Shadow
- No Different bullet points
- No Low contrast
- No Wrong mathematical symbol
- No Wrap
- No Centred block
- No Highlighting in UBS Red
