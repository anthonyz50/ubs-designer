# Action Stamp

> Make your message or promotion pop

To find the right size for an action stamp in the layout, compare the capital letters of the text with the height of the key symbol (k).

## Colour Options

| Text | Background |
|------|-----------|
| Black | Bronze I |
| White | Bronze II |
| White | Bordeaux I |
| White | Bordeaux II |
| Black | Gray I |
| Black | Gray II |
| Black | Gray III |
| White | Gray IV |

## Don't
- No low contrast
- No overlap
- No unbalanced proportions
- No cluttered placement
