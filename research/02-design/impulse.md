# Impulse

> The starting point of everything great

The Impulse is UBS's distinctive red vertical line that acts as a design cue alongside text messaging.

## Top Takeaways

1. The format determines the **width** of the Impulse.
2. The format determines the **space** between Impulse and text.
3. The height of the text determines the **height** of the Impulse.

---

## Width

The format determines the width of the Impulse, which is scaled according to the table below.

### ISO Format Impulse Width

| Format | Dimension (mm) | Stroke Width (pt) |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| A8 | 52 × 74 | 1.2 |
| A7 | 74 × 105 | 1.9 |
| A6 | 105 × 148 | 2.6 |
| A6/5 | 105 × 210 | 2.6 |
| A5 | 148 × 210 | 3.2 |
| A4 | 210 × 297 | 4.5 |
| A3 | 297 × 420 | 6.7 |
| A2 | 420 × 594 | 9.0 |
| A1 | 594 × 841 | 12.7 |
| A0 | 841 × 1189 | 18.0 |

If the format isn't defined yet, the width of the stroke is **twice the width of a key stalk**.

---

## Height

### Keyline Only
Impulse starts at the cap height of the first word and ends at the baseline of the last line. Always leave the last line blank.

### Keyline and Infoline
The Impulse starts at the cap height of the first word in the keyline and ends at the baseline of the infoline. Leave the first line of the infoline blank.

### Non-Latin Script
Specific rules for: Japanese, Chinese, Cyrillic, Arabic, Hebrew. Vertical alignment available for Japanese script.

---

## Space

The format determines the distance of the Impulse to the keyline and the infoline.

### ISO Format Impulse Space

| Format | Dimension (mm) | Space (mm) |
|--------|----------------|------------|
| A8 | 52 × 74 | 1.8 |
| A7 | 74 × 105 | 2.4 |
| A6 | 105 × 148 | 3.0 |
| A6/5 | 105 × 210 | 3.5 |
| A5 | 148 × 210 | 4.9 |
| A4 | 210 × 297 | 7.0 |
| A3 | 297 × 420 | 9.9 |
| A2 | 420 × 594 | 14.0 |
| A1 | 594 × 841 | 19.8 |
| A0 | 841 × 1189 | 28.0 |

If there is no defined format, the distance between the Impulse and the text is **half the height of the key symbol**.

---

## Range of Use

The Impulse can connect various elements to create something holistic. It acts as UBS's red thread and creates a flexible and recognisable design cue. It can also highlight specific information like numbers or a quote.

### Application contexts:
- Print media covers, digital media headers or social media posts
- Alternative for advertising (shorter Impulse only for special applications)
- Print and digital media content
- Social media posts

---

## Don't

- No longer than the baseline of the second message
- Don't end at the baseline of the keyline
- No multiple use together
- Don't centre with keyline, leaving the Impulse longer than the cap height
- Don't centre with text, leaving the Impulse longer than the cap height of the first and the baseline of the second message
- On Gray, Bordeaux or Bronze tones: Impulse allowed on Pastel I and Pastel II only
- No accent or highlighting of the infoline in UBS Red on front pages or on transparent Moving Frame
