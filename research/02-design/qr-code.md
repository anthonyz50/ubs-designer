# QR Code

> Bridges between the physical and digital world

## Size

Always use **short links** to ensure reasonable size and readability.

### Minimum QR Code Sizes by Format

| Format | Short (25 chars) mm | Long (125 chars) mm | Extensive (300 chars) mm |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| A5 | 10 | 20 | 30 |
| A4 | 25 | 45 | 65 |
| A3 | 35 | 70 | 90 |
| A2 | 50 | 100 | 145 |
| A1 | 90 | 170 | 250 |
| A0 | 140 | 260 | 380 |
| F4 | 180 | 340 | 500 |
| F200 | 230 | 430 | 630 |
| F12 | 280 | 520 | 760 |
| 12 m² | 370 | 690 | 1000 |

## Placement
- Always apply QR codes on a **surrounding white background**
- Align with margin of format or Moving Frame
- Can align with the Impulse

### Clear Space
Use **two square modules** (smallest single square of QR code) for clear space around the QR code.

## Campaign Tracking
