# Motion and Video

> Let the UBS branding play on with creative, clear, consistent and mobile-first video content.

## Top Takeaways

1. For video footage, follow the look of our imagery. Warm and welcoming with a human focus.
2. Whenever possible, optimise videos for mobile applications.
3. Relevant and creative content is key, but consistent brand elements make videos ownable for UBS. Same approach for external and internal videos.

---

## Overview of Elements

- Logo Tab animation
- Subtitle
- Text on footage / with Impulse
- Transparent lower third on image
- Opaque lower third on solid background
- Text image combination
- Text on white with Impulse
- Outro with URL / disclaimer

---

## Logo Tab

### Standard (Left-Aligned)
For animated content, use left-aligned Logo Tab animation near top-left corner. Watermark can remain at end of animation.

### Exception (Right-Aligned)
When important footage would be covered by left-aligned Logo Tab or overlapped by permanent navigation elements.

### Partnership Logo Tab
- Use horizontal partnership Logo Tab whenever possible
- Switch to vertical for extreme portrait formats
- Not recommended to have visible throughout video (occupies too much space)
- Watermark of both logos can remain after animation (unless transparent version doesn't work for partner logo)

## Transitions

- **Cut** (standard)
- **Dissolve**
- **Dip to white**
- **Pattern image to image**
- **Pattern colour to colour**
- **Pattern outline on colour I / II**

## Outro

Two versions: white background and on footage. Use the version that suits context best.

### Co-branding and Partnership
- Co-branding on white background
- Partnership logo on white background / on footage

## Specifications

### Formats
- 16:9, 9:16, 2:1, 1:1

### Subtitles
- When framing subject, ensure subtitles can be used
- White font on black background with **80% opacity** (template available)
- Provide option to turn on/off if possible
- Include subtitle track with narration/dialogue for hearing impaired
- Provide transcript for screen reader

### Endings
- Always use ending with **sound logo** (except silent videos)
- Always use **animated outro logo** (except assets <10 seconds)
- URL possible (see templates for position)

### Production Standards
- **Minimum:** Full HD
- **UBS standard:** 4K 25fps, 4K 50fps
- Separate language track and clean title backgrounds for multi-language
- Audio track in 5.1 recommended (stereo mandatory)

### File Formats
- h264, mp4, minimum CBR 6 mbps
- QuickTime non-compression

### Production Tools
- Adobe Premiere, Adobe After Effects

### Sound Levels
- **12dbfs / -23 LKFS** (standard EBU R128)
- **12dbfs / -16 LKFS** (standard mobile)

---

## Don't

- No centred text with more than two lines
- No marginal text box
- No undefined text effects
- No coloured text box
- No watermark on static background
- No detached logo on footage
- No additional logo on outro
- No bottom right aligned logo on film/video
- No additional logo animation
- No subtitles with more than two lines
- No blur effects
- No combining text box and Logo Tab
- No gradients
