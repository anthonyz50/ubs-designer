# Social Media

> Bring your designs to life on social media

## Top Takeaways

1. For **static content:** right-aligned Logo Tab (bottom-right corner). No logo on gallery uploads where cropped in preview. Logo on first and last image of slider.
2. For **animated content:** left-aligned Logo Tab animation with watermark (top-left corner) and logo outro with sound per motion design guidelines.
3. Follow the sample applications of each channel and note interactive elements (buttons). Design guidelines apply to all applications including thought-leadership content.

---

## Basics

### Principles
- Place the logo on the first and last slide
- For key visuals, pair logo and images with backgrounds in White or Pastel tones
- In intermediate slides, use Gray, Bordeaux, or Bronze tones as accent backgrounds

### Colour Directions

| Direction | Colours |
|-----------|---------|
| **Basis (Gray)** | Warm Gray, Pastel I |
| **Specific I (Bordeaux)** | Warm Gray, Bordeaux accents, Pastel I or Pastel II |
| **Specific II (Bronze)** | Warm Gray, Bronze accents, Pastel II |

### Big Picture
- Ensure balanced overall appearance through basic colour scheme
- Show variety by using layout options
- Set individual accents through contrasts and careful image selection

## Don't

- No cropped Logo Tab in image gallery
- No watermark on static content
- No Logo Tab on multiple images
- No overlay of permanent navigation elements
- No collage layouts
- No source connected to the Impulse
