# Event Experience

> Putting our positive and consistent brand experience in the spotlight

## Top Takeaways

1. Bring brand personality alive; create holistic, immersive brand experience (physical, digital, hybrid).
2. Consider **material, light, furniture and motion** as integral parts of event design.
3. Maximum flexibility to tailor events according to specific context.

---

## Material

Swiss heritage inspires material choices:
- **Alpine Forest:** Wood
- **Mountains:** Earthen material
- **Fresh blanket of snow:** Fabric
- **Precious metal**
- **Glaciers:** Glass
- **Biodiversity:** Local plants

### Flooring Options
- PVC roll, stone, warm gray
- Carpet, recycled SDPET, warm gray
- PVC roll, oak
- Carpet, ribbed needle felt, bordeaux

---

## Light

Use lighting to shape mood and create professional, engaging events.

- **Direct lighting** (spotlights): highlight speakers, branding, key areas
- **Indirect lighting** (wall lights, soft lamps): warm, balanced atmosphere

### Lighting Mood References
- **Morning/daytime:** Bright, energetic
- **Dusk:** Warm, transitional
- **Evening/nighttime:** Intimate, sophisticated

---

## Colours (Events)

### Key Symbol on Coloured Backgrounds

## Don't (Events)
- No mixing materials within same category
- No mixing Bordeaux and Bronze
- No combining different patterns
- No cold atmosphere
- No mixing furniture styles

---

## Sponsorship Communication Options

| Sponsorship Type | Communication Style |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Main sponsorship | UBS communication style (preferred) |
| Co-main sponsorship | Partner or UBS communication |
| Co-sponsorship | Partner communication |
| Presenting sponsorship | UBS as presenting sponsor with descriptor |
| Title sponsorship | UBS is primary/sole sponsor |

### Co-branding vs Composite Logo
- **Co-branding:** One-off partnerships
- **Composite logo:** Strategic partnerships only (requires Group Brand Experience approval)

---

## Gifts and Packaging

- Overall appearance: **white**
- High quality and sustainable products/materials
- Logo placed at least once per gift or package
- **Bottom right** is standard logo position
- Centred position possible depending on item shape
- Pattern for special cases

### Don't (Gifts)
- No functional items
- No food
- No inappropriate or degrading products
- No items with security risk
