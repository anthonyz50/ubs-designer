# UBS Product Illustrations — Complete Guide

Source: UBS Frontify Document 365 — Design basics & Brand — Illustrations  
Contacts: Ewa Grajek (ewa.grajek@ubs.com), Joanna Suska (joanna.suska@ubs.com)

## Purpose

Illustrations establish a human touch within digital applications while considering specific target group and channel needs.

## Principles

- **Functional** — use an illustration for a purpose, not for decoration
- **Delightful** — bring warmth and personality
- **Digital** — designed for screen use
- **Consistent** — maintain consistent look and feel across all illustrations
- **Inclusive** — diverse characters, avoid clichés

## Categories

There are 6 defined categories plus a special "City" category:

### 01 Spot (Free to use)
Simple object-focused illustrations. No people or minimal human elements. Used for features, products, status states, and informational contexts.

**Examples:** access_app_confirmation, building_skyscraper, card_payment_generic, coins_money, compass, document_chart, flowers_butterfly, globe_currency, graph_board, house_building, piggy_bank_butterfly, sailboat, shield_security_protection, umbrella_insurance

**Count:** ~140+ illustrations

### 02 Scene (Free to use)
Character-driven illustrations showing people in context. Used for onboarding, welcome screens, lifestyle moments, and feature promotion.

**Examples:** man_water_plant, woman_walk_dog_phone_gold, digital_banking_couple_coffee_phone_XL, woman_climbing_mountain, man_telescope, woman_bicycle, kids_banking_boy_girl_savings

**Count:** ~100+ illustrations

### 03 Case Related (UX Patterns only)
Reserved for specific UX pattern use cases. More narrative and context-specific than Spot or Scene.

**Examples:** air_balloon, astronaut_stars, clocks, rocket_fly_success, scissors, woman_sailing_boat_lake, tree_miss_page_questionmark_pin_404 (404 page), spinner

**Count:** ~38 illustrations

### 04 Project Related (Restricted usage)
**Product designers and Product Owners usage rights only.** Reserved category representing product storytelling. Check Frontify metadata for usage rights.

**Examples:** eBill, qr_bill_upload, qr_code_invoice, PFM, graph_flowers_investment, man_globe_trade, twint_registration

**Count:** ~53 illustrations

### 05 Explanatory (Instructional)
Step-by-step and instructional illustrations for onboarding flows, setup processes, identity verification, and feature explanation.

**Examples:** NFC_ID_scan, passport_camera_checkmark, phone_biometry_fingerprint, Confirm_identity, access_app_phone_sms_code, access_card_login, phone_camera_selfie, Smartphone_login_biometrics_padlock

**Count:** ~90 illustrations

### 06 Thumbnail (Simplified, smaller format)
Simplified, compact versions of illustrations for message boxes, lists, and smaller UI contexts.

**Examples:** thumbnail_bell_notification, thumbnail_coins, thumbnail_house, thumbnail_lightbulb, thumbnail_padlock_security, thumbnail_piggybank_butterfly, thumbnail_sailboat, thumbnail-investments-chart

**Count:** ~102 illustrations

### City (Special category)
Landmark-based city skyline illustrations for 79 global cities. Available in both SVG and PNG formats. Used for location-based features, office pages, and market context.

**Cities include:** Amsterdam, Auckland, Bangalore, Bangkok, Basel, Beijing, Berlin, Bogota, Boston, Brisbane, Cape Town, Chicago, Copenhagen, Delhi, Dubai, Frankfurt, Geneva, Hong Kong, Houston, Jakarta, Krakow, Kuala Lumpur, Las Vegas, Lisbon, London, Los Angeles, Lugano, Madrid, Melbourne, Mexico City, Miami, Milan, Mumbai, Munich, Nashville, New York City, Osaka, Paris, Perth, San Francisco, Santiago, Sao Paulo, Seoul, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Singapore, Stockholm, Sydney, Taipei, Tel Aviv, Tokyo, Toronto, Washington DC, Wellington, Yokohama, Zurich, and more.

**Count:** 79 cities (SVG + PNG = 158 files)

## Size and Scale

### Standard Illustrations
- **Default size:** 279 × 224px
- **Aspect ratio:** 5:4
- **Scaling range:** 70–130% (for mobile screens and E-Banking)
  - Minimum (70%): 195 × 157px
  - Maximum (130%): 363 × 291px
- **Important:** Use the "Scale" tool in Sketch/Figma to prevent deformation or automatic pixel alignment

### Usage Contexts at Standard Size
- Welcome screen
- Onboarding screen guidance
- Risk profile results
- Overlay
- Teaser E-Banking

### Thumbnail with Message Box
- **Size:** 80 × 80px
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1
- **Scaling range:** 56 × 56px (min) to 112 × 112px (max)
- For other sizes, request from the illustration team

## Style Rules (from illustrations.json)

- Hand-drawn characteristics created digitally
- Firm pen stroke (minimum 1px digital)
- Abstract where possible, accurate where appropriate
- Human proportions: small head, short torso, long legs, large feet (NOT realistic)
- No facial expressions
- Dominant colour is always white
- Colour layering order: black lines → red accent lines → red fill → black fill → warm grays fill
- Additional colour (Bordeaux II #8A000A) at ~10% for large/complex illustrations only
- No overuse of colours, no too-many filled areas, no other colours beyond palette
- No too-thin/thick/rigid/wobbly lines
- No too-many details, no too-cartoonish, no obvious 3D
- No combination with photography
- No illustrations as icons (they are separate systems)
- **Always default to SVG format** — use PNG only as fallback when SVG is unavailable
- No close-ups, no sketchy style
