# Pattern

> Reveal our signature touch

The UBS pattern, inspired by the key symbol, is used exclusively to elevate the perceived value of selected assets. Applied only when it enhances the premium character of the design.

## Top Takeaways

1. The pattern comes in four versions: **solid or outline, static or animated**. Always use within defined minimum and maximum size ratios.
2. **Gray and pastel** tones form the foundation. **Bordeaux** and **Bronze** for accents selectively.
3. Carefully assess whether the context and application create a premium impression before using the pattern.

---

## Ratio

### Format
Always use the short side of the format to measure the ratio. Reference: height (h) of the base element.
- **Maximum size:** 3h
- **Minimum size:** ⅓h

When combining pattern with other objects, ensure good contrast ratio.

## Context and Usage

To create a premium impression using patterns, focus on: **touchpoint, context, contrast, and colour**.

### Subtle Applications:
Privacy screen, Tote, Blanket, Water bottle, Video backdrop, Sculpture, Pillow, Leather case, Stanley cup, Moodwall (wooden/LED)

### Bold Applications:
Foulard, Video backdrop, Access card, Gift wrap, Giveaway bag, Invitation

---

## Don't

- No distortion
- No Logo Tab on white background
- No Key Symbol on pattern
- No wrong format ratio
- No front page and Impulse on pattern
- No multiple variations
- No mirroring
- No background for portrait photography
- No other colours
- No combination of two colour directions
- No rotation
- No icon, illustration or chart on pattern
- No bleeding on one, two or three sides
- No wrong colour contrast
