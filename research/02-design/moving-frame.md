# Moving Frame

> Give essential information a dynamic space

## Top Takeaways

1. The Moving Frame comes in two versions: **transparent** for all images and **opaque** for monochrome backgrounds or when transparency isn't possible.
2. The standard position and dimensions are based on the Logo Tab. For all spacing and margins, we use **half the height of the Key Symbol (K)**.
3. The Moving Frame measures a **maximum of half the height and width of the format**. We align it with the right margin at a variable height.

---

## Opacity

### Transparent (standard), opacity 80%
Used on pictures.

### Opaque, opacity 100%
- Used on patterns
- Used on monochrome backgrounds and when the transparent Moving Frame is not technically possible

---

## Dimension

We build the Moving Frame based on the Logo Tab. For all spacing and margins, we use half the height of the key symbol.

The Moving Frame can also function as a text box.

---

## Size

For full effect, the Moving Frame must be recognisable as a dynamic element. Therefore:
- **Portrait and square**: Never larger than half the width
- **Landscape**: Never larger than half the height

---

## Floating

To place the Moving Frame on an image, we align it with the margin at a variable height.

---

## Don't

- No centred placement
- No left-aligned logo
- No framed version
- No wrong logo position
- No rotating
- No bleeding to the edge
- No highlighting in UBS Red
- No transparency on monochrome background
- No transparency on patterns
- No opacity lower than 80%
