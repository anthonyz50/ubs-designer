# Disclaimer

> Terms and conditions may apply

## Top Takeaways

1. Consider whether a static or interactive application area is intended and the scope of the disclaimer.
2. For font size, position and content, check country-specific rules with local legal and compliance.
3. Use the **height of the key symbol** as unit of measurement for alignment.

---

## Static

### Full-Bleed Image
- Only place disclaimer directly on image if text is clearly distinguishable and legible
- **Minimum font size: 8 pt**
- If contrast ratio is insufficient, place white area behind disclaimer

### White Background
- With secondary layout option: place disclaimer on white background
- Full white background: place disclaimer on white background

## Interactive
- When permitted, place a link directing to relevant disclaimer
- Check with Legal and C&ORC for compliance

## Legal Descriptor
- Align short legal notices with **upper-right corner of margin**
