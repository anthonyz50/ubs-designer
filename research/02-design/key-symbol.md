# Key Symbol

> Ensure brand impact, leverage the UBS Key Symbol

Historically, the Key Symbol was designed in 1937 and has become an iconic and well recognized logo mark over the decades. As a standalone brand asset, it unlocks opportunities for impact: when integrated across a wide range of touchpoints, it enhances recognition, deepening emotional connections with our clients.

**Note:** Usage of the Key Symbol on its own is permitted only in environments that are already branded UBS. For unique solutions beyond what is defined here, contact Group Brand Experience for guidance and usage approval.

## Top Takeaways

1. The Key Symbol can be used in various ways as a standalone element to drive brand impact.
2. It can be used in static and motion formats. Available as standalone, tab, watermark and 3D animation.
3. **It should never be altered, deconstructed, or separated into individual components** (e.g. using one or more keys in isolation).

---

## Standalone

The Key Symbol can appear across a wide range of applications, from digital interfaces and printed materials to merchandise, spatial branding, and event environments.

When placed directly on imagery, choose visuals that are calm and uncluttered to ensure clarity and legibility.

## Clear Space

The clear space is defined by unit of **k** (the height of the Key Symbol).

**Minimal padding of the Key Symbol is ⅓k.**

## Colour

Preferred colours for the Key Symbol are **black** or **white**; in select cases, **Gray** may be used to achieve a softer contrast. **Bordeaux** and **Bronze** can also be used (as seen in the 3D section and the event chapter).

### Colour Combinations:
- Black on white
- White on black
- Warm Gray on white
- Black on picture
- White on picture
- Warm Gray I on Warm Gray III

## Key Symbol Tab

We generally use the **bottom right-aligned** version of our Key Symbol Tab. If this is not possible due to technical or other reasons, we use the **top left-aligned** version.

Inside the Key Symbol Tab, the Key Symbol is **always black**.

Specifications available for:
- Bottom right Key Symbol Tab construction
- Top left Key Symbol Tab construction

## Key Symbol Without Tab

When the Key Symbol is used without the respective tab, its placement should follow the established grid system.

## Watermark

Enhance brand visibility and credibility across various touchpoints with the Key Symbol watermark. It reinforces brand identity and recognition across static or moving imagery.

### Placement options:
- Centred
- Bottom right
- Top left

## Motion Usage

### Intro / Outro
Motion and moving imagery drive higher engagement. The Key Symbol can be a dynamic and engaging feature to transition in and out of video footage.

### Transition
There is an option to leverage the Key Symbol to transition from one footage to the next.

## Best Practice

- Ensure sufficient clear space around the standalone Key Symbol
- In cluttered or complex visual environments, avoid using the standalone Key Symbol
- In unfamiliar markets, always lead with full UBS Logo
- Do not combine more than one asset per surface or scene (e.g. 3D + watermark)
- When using a motion asset (e.g. 3D animation or intro animation), it takes precedence, no additional motion branding should be layered

### Customisation
For usage of the Key Symbol not covered here, consult Group Brand Experience before implementation. All non-standard usage require review and approval.

---

## Don't

- No Unapproved colours
- No Understated usage
- No Low contrast
- No Shadow
- No Outline
- No Rotation
