# Imagery

> Warm and human – the UBS picture style

## Top Takeaways

1. We create a human look and feel with **warm light and golden tones**.
2. We capture people's everyday lives, the simple natural pleasures that surround them and the aspirations that drive them.
3. We show people in a positive context and atmosphere, aiming for authenticity, while conveying true emotions.

---

## Subject

### People and Portraits
- Authentic and charismatic
- Natural beauty and elegance of diverse people from all over the world
- Active, ambitious, optimistic, bright and confident, genuine and real people

### Objects
- Every object tells something about the people we portray
- Every object forms a natural part of a person's life

### Situations
- Vivid, upbeat, confident, optimistic and active people thriving in their lives
- People exploring new possibilities: ideas, experiences, business ventures, people, places and things
- People interacting in a natural and positive way at work, at home, in public or with friends

### Locations – Urban
- Showing contemporary flair
- Spacious, tidy, full of light and warmth, invites viewer to become part of the location

### Locations – Nature
- Showing the beauty of originality and authenticity
- Immersive, draws viewer in to explore and linger

### Technology and Innovation
- Forward-thinking people who are curious by nature
- People celebrating the latest technology and innovation
- People shaping the future for the better in a genuine and contemporary way

### Don't (Subject)
- No fashion/beauty model and extreme make-up
- No staged objects
- No staged scenes
- No dark or unwelcoming environments

## Technique

### Contrast and Light
- The time of day and the warmth of the light are part of everyday life
- From morning to evening, the light and atmosphere shift, allowing selection of the right mood for each context
- **Morning / Afternoon / Evening** moods
- Use contrasts to emphasise light and warmth

### Colour and Accents
- Use warm greys and pastels from the colour palette as subtle base accents
- Add distinctive touch with highlights in Bordeaux or Bronze

#### Colour Basis Types:
- **Basis**: Warm Gray + Pastel
- **Specific I**: Warm Gray + Pastel + Bordeaux accents
- **Specific II**: Warm Gray + Pastel + Bronze accents

Compare image colours with the secondary colour palette to check alignment.

### Depth of Focus
- Sharp focus on what matters most
- Background or foreground elements may appear softly blurred

### Perspective
- Choose perspective thoughtfully to support the story
- Natural and authentic viewpoints are preferred
- When showing people, align with the protagonist's eye level

### Don't (Technique)
- No lighting/flash, low contrast
- No wrong colour atmosphere
- No shallow depth of field
- No special lenses, harsh perspectives
- No black silhouettes
- No blurry, flat, cheesy
- No dominant colour
- No mixed contrast
- No colourful (oversaturated)

## Checklist

### Focus
- Emphasise the aspects of the brand personality
- Show priorities through focal points and depth of focus

### Composition
- Look at the subject and colours of surroundings
- Think about the perspective

### Consistency
- Select series or shootings or stylistically similar images
- Find accents
- Pay attention to contrasts and colourway

### Implementation
- Check the format and ratio of the touchpoint and application
- Consider the scope of texts and design elements

### Context
- Think about where the image will be used: single touchpoint or user journey

---

## Retouching

- Alter pictures as little as possible
- Colour grading can make a picture feel warmer, friendlier, and give the right contrast
- Sometimes only need to adjust specific parts (partial colour grading)
- Pictures must always look authentic and natural
- Some pictures won't work with the warm style and editing can't fix them (pictures with lots of white/grey, dark backgrounds, very little colour, or harsh cold lighting)
