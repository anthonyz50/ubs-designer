# Sender Information

> Hello, who is this? It's us. Make sure the recipient knows right away. Simple does it, less is more.

## Local Customs
- Always write the address in the local language and according to local customs
- Check local post regulations for correct addressing

---

## Categories

Four categories of sender information, from minimal to detailed:

### 1. Poster
General and as reduced as possible (e.g. on posters)

### 2. Brochure
Local contact information (e.g. on brochures, newsletters, invitations)

### 3. Letter
Complete contact and personal information (e.g. on letters)

### 4. Business Card
Personal contact information

## Endorsement

Mandatory for all subsidiaries to indicate relation to UBS AG.
- Not for subsidiaries of subsidiaries
- Not for UBS AG and UBS SA (subsidiaries only)

| Language | Endorsement Line |
|----------|-----------------|
| EN | (Legal name) is a subsidiary of UBS AG |
| DE | (Legal name) ist eine Tochtergesellschaft von UBS AG |
| FR | (Legal name) est une filiale d'UBS SA |
| IT | (Legal name) è una società affiliata di UBS SA |
| ES | (Legal name) es una filial de UBS AG |

## Phone Numbers

### Format Rules
- Hyphen after country code and area code
- Space between digit groups

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| International | +41-61-288 75 39 |
| National | 061-288 75 39 |
| Internal | 1921-87539 |
| Free | 0800 800 999 |
