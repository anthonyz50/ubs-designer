# Dark Mode

> Our corporate colours are at the heart of our brand – no matter which mode you use.

Dark Mode flips the usual look: background turns dark, text becomes light. It's a feature built into most operating systems (iOS, Android), and clients can switch it on whenever they like.

**Why clients use it:** Reduces eye strain, makes reading easier in low light.

**Think of Dark Mode as an extra option, not the default.** This keeps the brand experience consistent for everyone.

---

## Colours for Dark Mode

Two new shades of red instead of the standard UBS Red. These colours stand out clearly on dark backgrounds.

### UBS Dark Mode Primary Red
- **HEX:** #D83B31
- **RGB:** 216, 59, 49

Applied to key brand and interface elements: logo, buttons, larger areas (illustrations, charts).

**Note:** For illustrations, lines and coloured areas need to be adjusted for Dark Mode.

### UBS Dark Mode Secondary Red
- **HEX:** #FE6F5D
- **RGB:** 254, 111, 93

Used for highlights, text links, accents, secondary interaction elements, or lines.

---

## Charts and Graphs

- Colours in donut, bar and area charts remain the **same in Light and Dark Mode**
- Gaps (separator lines) must show as **white lines**
- UBS Dark Mode Primary Red can highlight a segment in Dark Mode
- For line, scatter, radar and other charts (where separator lines can't be shown), only colours with **enough contrast on dark background** can be used
