# Messaging

> What do we tell – facts, information, a story? Either way, we write clear and convincing. And if applicable, we add charm.

## Catchy Keyline, Informative Infoline

Our message principle is like a 1-2 punch, with a **big catchy keyline** and a **smaller informative infoline**.

---

## Communication Style

From factual to communicative:

### 1. Just the Facts
- Infoline black only

### 2. Facts Plus / Catchy
- Keyline hooks, infoline explains

### 3. Communicative
- Tells a story

### Don't
- Don't alter the defined sizes for keyline and infoline (see Typography)
- Don't use single, wordy titles. Always stick to the tone of voice: clear, convincing, and with charm

---

## Highlight

**Important:** To meet accessibility standards and maintain consistency across all touchpoints, we **no longer have the red highlight** in our message (infoline or keyline).

### Don't
- No highlighting in UBS Red
