# Design Introduction

> Welcome to the UBS Design Guidelines

These guidelines ensure a consistent and recognisable brand presence across all touchpoints worldwide.

## Document Structure

The Design section covers:

### Introduction and Quick Guide
- Quick Guide: Overview of all brand elements
- Best Practice: Examples of correct brand application
- Certified Brand Partners: Approved agencies

### Basic Elements
1. **Logo** - Three black keys, three red capital letters
2. **Key Symbol** - The iconic key symbol as standalone brand asset
3. **Typography** - Frutiger typeface system
4. **Colours** - Corporate, secondary, and special-use colours
5. **Imagery** - Warm and human picture style
6. **Impulse** - The red vertical line design cue
7. **Moving Frame** - Dynamic space for information
8. **Layout** - Grid system and element relationships
9. **Icons** - Illustrative and web app icons
10. **Illustrations** - Hand-drawn style illustrations
11. **Pattern** - Key symbol-inspired patterns
12. **Messaging** - Keyline and infoline system
13. **Sender Information** - Address and contact formatting
14. **Sound** - Sound logo and branded music
