# Quick Guide

> Welcome! This quick guide is the perfect introduction for new joiners and anyone looking to explore our brand, especially creatives.

Templates include correct logo sizes, margins, colours, and more, making it easy to create UBS materials. Start by reading the guidelines thoroughly, then check out the Downloads section.

---

## Brand Personality

- We are **positive, warm and human**: optimistic by nature, listening to clients' unique needs
- We are **forward looking and empowering**: contemporary with purposeful intelligence, inspiring connections

---

## Brand Design

**The same, worldwide.** Our brand is positive, forward looking, empowering, and warm and human. These qualities define and influence our design. We consistently use the same framework with fixed and flexible basic elements. This approach is applied worldwide and is not optional.

---

## Logo
**Our greatest asset.** Three black keys and three red capital letters. Trademark protected worldwide.

## Layout
**Frame or tab?** The layout ensures brand consistency. The **Moving Frame** (preferred) offers dynamic space with full-bleed image. The **Logo Tab** gives the logo a stage to shine when applied to a full-bleed image or an image bleeding off two sides.

## Impulse
**Attention, please!** The red vertical line that draws attention to important information. Typically used with keyline and infoline.

## Typography
**Friendly Frutiger.** Clear, crisp, and welcoming. Combines modern aesthetic with timeless quality.

## Messaging
**Catchy and informative.** Big catchy keyline followed by smaller informative infoline.

## Colours
**Hue harmony.** Corporate colours: lots of white, black, and a little bit of red. Secondary colours serve a function: to add warmth or highlight intriguing content.

## Imagery
**Warm and human.** Capture snapshots of people's everyday lives, aiming for authenticity and positivity. Thousands of pictures in the UBS Image Database.

## Illustrations
**Crafted by hand.** Used when we can't say it with an image. Enable connections, enhance storytelling, visualise complex topics.

## Icons
**Less is more.** Used sparingly, when they make sense in context. Standard: black with red accent. Also available in plain black.

## Pattern
**Simply premium.** Inspired by the key symbol. Only applied on carefully selected items or in video. Evokes a premium signature feel.

## Sound
**Shh, listen.** Sound logo and branded music pieces add depth and recognition. The animated sound logo combines subtle animation with sound.
