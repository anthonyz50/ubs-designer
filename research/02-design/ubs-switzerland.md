# UBS Switzerland

- Pattern (Swiss-specific)
- Brand naming (Swiss-specific)
- Email endorsement (Swiss-specific)

- Media booking

---

## Media Booking

Key guidelines for all media bookings in Switzerland:

1. **Publicis Media** (globally appointed agency) must be informed about ALL media bookings, even if booked directly by business segment
2. Full transparency required to avoid double bookings
3. **Print publications:** Max 2 insertions per publication/title, not exceeding 1.5 pages total
4. **Online media:** Minimum booking period of 2 weeks consecutive
5. Publicis Media distributes booking forecast lists:
   - Online: monthly
   - Print: quarterly
6. UBS eScreens (own channels) only for self-explanatory messages for broader public
