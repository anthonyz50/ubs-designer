# Illustrations

> With human elements and hand-drawn characteristics, we show our approachable personality.

## Top Takeaways

1. With hand drawn characteristics and small imperfection, we create a **human and welcoming** look and feel.
2. We aim for **reduction and clarity**. Abstract where possible, accurate where appropriate. We strive for the essence of things.
3. We highlight things or aspects that are truly relevant for the message.

---

## Why We Use Illustrations

- **Differentiation:** Our Illustrations differentiate us from competitors
- **Connection:** Illustrations create clear visual connections and enable individually positioning subjects
- **Storytelling:** Illustrations are great storytellers, displaying information clearly and vividly
- **Visualisation:** Especially to visualise specific or complex information when suitable images are hard to find

---

## How We Use Illustrations

- Use purposefully, don't overuse. If we can say the same with an image, we use an image
- Use appropriately to media, channel and target groups
- Hand drawn characteristics and small imperfections create a human look. But not too sketchy or comic style
- Professional look and proper style. Don't use illustrations as icons

---

## What Makes a UBS Illustration

- **Human:** Real everyday scenes with a human element (person, body part or personified object). Personality, humour and wit.
- **Modern:** Up-to-date, contemporary without being trendy. Representing society in all its facets.
- **Different:** Unified look and feel. Immediately recognisable by innovative use of colours and lively representation.

---

## Look and Feel

Simple and clear **black outlines**. Little additional colour to carefully emphasise details or set accents.

### Colour Layering Order:
1. Black lines with accent Red lines
2. Black lines, with accent Red fill
3. Black lines, Red accent fill, Black fill
4. Black lines, Red accent fill, Black and Warm Grays fill

**White must always be used predominantly.**

---

## Drawing Style

All drawings incorporate hand drawn characteristics, created digitally. Avoid too straight lines, always include small imperfections.

### Line Weight
Drawing lines are always firm and correspond to a regular pen stroke in any displayed size. Minimum 1px in digital context. Optically adjust line weight when scaling.

### Level of Detail
- Abstract where possible, accurate where appropriate
- Clearly defined forms, grasp the essence of things
- Highlight things truly relevant for the message
- Create dimensions by layering

### Human Proportions
People are **not drawn in realistic proportions**: small head, short torso, long legs, large feet.

---

## Use of Colour

Standard use: corporate colours and Warm Grays. In most illustrations no extra colours needed. If necessary, **Bordeaux II** can be used as additional colour (approximately 10% of the illustration).

### Corporate Colours
- Black
- UBS Red
- White

### Warm Grays
- Warm Gray I through VII

### Additional Colours
- **Bordeaux II**: For large/complex illustrations, to emphasise more details, if additional accents are needed

---

## Technical Advice

- Use pencil tool in Adobe Illustrator (or similar) for hand drawn look
- Drawing tablet (Wacom or similar) strongly recommended
- Alternative: vectors with pen tool, then add selective imperfections manually
- Key: firm and stable stroke, avoid shakiness
- Wrinkle Tool in Adobe Illustrator for selective imperfections

---

## Animated Illustrations

- Reflect natural, human-like qualities
- Avoid stiffness or cartoonish movements

### Practical Tips:
- Use 2D animation to maintain simple, flat illustration style
- Apply effects that don't disrupt shapes or line thickness
- Animate only what's essential for a clean, elegant result
- Minimal animations in complex compositions to minimise overwhelm
- Set a pace ensuring the message is clear
- Vary speed with easing for realistic movement
- Add subtle boiling line effect for hand-drawn charm

---

## Don't

### Colours:
- No overuse of colours
- No too many filled areas
- No other colours beyond the palette

### Lines:
- No too thin and rigid
- No too thick and rigid
- No too wobbly

### Miscellaneous:
- No combination of different line widths
- No too many details and lines
- No too cartoonish / different human proportions
- No filled with background colour
- No close-ups
- No facial expressions
- No obvious 3D (use layering instead)
- No combination with photographic elements
- No illustrations used as icons
- No too sketchy

### Animations:
- No disruption of shape
- No cartoonish movement
- No overcomplicated animation
