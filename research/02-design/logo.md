# Logo

> Three black keys, three red capital letters and infinite potential

## Logo Overview

The logo is protected worldwide as a UBS trademark.

### Key Symbol Alone
To be used only in a clearly UBS-branded environment.

### No Additional Logos
Creating logos or labels for products, services, teams, organizational units or applications is not permitted. The only exceptions are special identities for title sponsorships.

**Reference:** [UBS logo licensing](internal users only)

For guidance on any aspect of logo usage, contact the brand expert of your business division through the branding network (internal users only).

---

## Logo Tab

Our Logo Tab gives our logo room to shine. It is a white, calm background that ensures contrast and visibility.

### Top Takeaways

1. Our Logo Tab exists in two standard versions. Each version is bound to the appropriate application.
2. The right-aligned Logo Tab is the standard version and the size of the Logo Tab is defined by the page format.
3. We align the Logo Tab to the page margin and keep a height of the Key Symbol (k) distance from the key in the Logo Tab to the upper or the lower edge of the format.

We generally use the **bottom right-aligned** version of our Logo Tab. If this is not possible due to technical reasons, we use the **top left-aligned** version.

---

## Size

To place the logo on applications, we always use the provided files and scale the logo from its original size using an appropriate percentage value.

The value for the height of the logo depends on format and size.

### ISO Format Logo Sizes

| Format | Dimension (mm) | Logo Size (%) |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| A8 | 52 × 74 | 10 |
| A7 | 74 × 105 | 15 |
| A6 | 105 × 148 | 18 |
| A6/5 | 105 × 210 | 21 |
| A5 | 148 × 210 | 21 |
| A4 | 210 × 297 | 26 |
| A3 | 297 × 420 | 37 |
| A2 | 420 × 594 | 52 |
| A1 | 594 × 841 | 74 |
| A0 | 841 × 1189 | 104 |

To place the logo on an undefined format, use the short side of the format to determine the height of the logo.

### Orientation-based sizing:
- Ultra landscape
- Extended landscape
- Landscape
- Square and portrait
- Extended portrait
- Ultra portrait

---

## Position

1. Firstly, align the logo to the page margin.
2. Secondly, keep a height of the Key Symbol (k) distance from the key in the Logo Tab to the upper or the lower edge of the format.

In the case of a white background, we place the Logo Tab the same way as on an image.

If you use the Chinese logo, simply apply the rules above.

- **Short**: Simplified (left/right-aligned)

## Co-branding

The co-branding rules apply on one-off partnerships, where it's important to distinguish the partnered brands.

### Placement Rules:
- Co-branding logo is left-aligned at the bottom and visually balanced
- UBS Logo Tab is placed as a sender right-aligned at the bottom
- In exceptional cases and on office applications: UBS logo at top left, co-branding logo at bottom right

### Hierarchy:
- Where UBS has the lead, UBS brand design is adopted
- Where a partner has the lead, the partner's brand design is adopted

### Contract before proceeding:
- Secure a contract that regulates all aspects of the visual appearance (including use of brand name and logo) and its implementation
- Where such contract does not exist, a licensing agreement is required

### Layout Variants:
- Standard
- Moving Frame (recommended)
- On image
- Special layout
- Without image

### Additional Elements
Depending on the context and the message, additional elements can be placed aligned with the Impulse or the partner logo.

## Partnership Logo

We create and use a partnership logo only for long-term strategic partnerships. The × symbol from the official template is used to represent the collaboration in a modern and contemporary way and is the preferred format.

Always involve Brand Management early on when creating partnership logos. Partnership logos require approval from Group Brand Experience (internal users only).

### Variants:
- Partnership logo (left-aligned / right-aligned)
- Partnership logo vertical (left-aligned / right-aligned)

If necessary, adjust the × symbol during design to ensure it appears optically centered between the logos.

### Descriptor
The partnership logo provides the possibility to include a text descriptor. Place the descriptor centered above or below one or both logos, depending on the information.

### Extension
For both partnership logo versions, we can extend the logo tab according to the size of the partner logo (Horizontal / Vertical).

---

## Composite Logo

The composite logo is used for more formal, business-oriented partnerships and in cases where the 'X' symbol appears visually unbalanced.

Use of composite logos requires approval from the Group Brand Experience team.

### Variants:
- Composite logo (left-aligned / right-aligned)
- Composite logo vertical (left-aligned)

### Descriptor
The composite logo provides the possibility to include a text descriptor. Place centered above or below one or both logos.

For both composite logo versions, we can extend the horizontal logo according to the size of the partner logo.

---

## Detached Logo

While our Logo Tab is the standard, we may need to use our logo without the tab in some applications. In this case, we use **half the height of the Key Symbol** to determine the clear space.

We use the extra bold logo for signage on media viewed from a distance of **15 meters or more**.

### Bold Variants:
- **Colored**: Standard for email signature, intranet, website
- **Black**: Print and sponsoring
- **White**: Fabric and sponsoring
- **Watermark**: Animation and video

### Extra Bold Variants:
- **Colored I**: On light backgrounds
- **Colored II**: For spatial applications and on dark backgrounds
- **White**: Special use on dark backgrounds
- **Black**: Special use on light backgrounds

### Material Applications:
- **Paper**: Embossing
- **Wood**: Carving
- **Metal**: Engraving
- **Fabric**: Embroidery
- **Foil**: Print
