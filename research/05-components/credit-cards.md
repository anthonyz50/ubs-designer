# UBS Standard Components: Credit Cards

> Source: UBS Frontify Portal, Standard Components (Document 17)

## Important
These are VISUAL INTERPRETATIONS of physical cards for ONLINE USE ONLY. For physical card guidelines, contact Brand Design Team.

## Anatomy
1. UBS logo (card-issuing bank)
2. Card type
3. Payment network logo (Mastercard, Visa)
4. Credit Card Number
5. Cardholder name
6. Card colour
7. Available limit

## Types
- **Default size** - 328px width, max 452px x 276px
- **Card icon** - miniature representation

## Card Backgrounds
Combination of colours and watermarks.
- Each colour chosen for specific context
- Special signs distinguish virtual cards
- UBS Key4 cards have special logo and background

## Cards Overview
- Visa / Mastercard (Physical + Virtual)
- Business and Corporate
- UBS Key4 (Prepaid, Standard, Premium)
- VPay / Maestro
- Lodge & Buyer's Card

## Grid (Stacking)
- **Big cards** - stack vertically, 56px visible from card below
- **Small card icons** - stack horizontally, 2px white space between, 30px visible from right
