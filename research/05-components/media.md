# UBS Standard Components: Media

> Source: UBS Frontify Portal, Standard Components (Document 17)

## Advertising
Refer to Brand Design Platform for digital ad guidance. Contact styleguide@ubs.com for in-app advertising.

## Audio
- UBS Sound Logo: for use at end of videos/recorded audio
- Music licensing: see Brand Design Platform Acoustics page
- No substantial app audio guidance yet
- Contact styleguide@ubs.com for audio feedback or text-to-voice projects

## Images

### Photography
Refer to Brand Design Platform for photography and illustration style guidance.

### Text on Images
- UBS design should be clear and easy to read
- Text on photographs: choose composition with plain dark/light background space
- NEVER place text on transparencies or gradients on top of images
