# UBS Logo Guidelines

> Source: UBS Brand Design Platform, Logo page

## The UBS Logo

The UBS logo consists of two elements:

- **Key symbol** — stands for the benefits we offer clients
- **Brand name** — represents our heritage as a company

The UBS logo is used by all business divisions, Corporate Center and all UBS subsidiaries.

### Core Rules

1. **The key symbol and brand name always appear together as a single unit**
2. In digital applications, the key symbol should **not** be used on its own as an icon, avatar, button, or decoration
3. **One exception:** Favicons (see below)

---

## Colours

In digital applications:

- **Key symbol:** Always black
- **Brand name:** Always UBS Red
- **Background:** Always white

**Do not use single-colour versions of the logo.**

---

## Weight

- In digital applications, **always use the semi-bold logo**
- Bold and extra-bold weights should **not** be used

---

## Size and Position

- Sizes are defined relative to the format used (see Layout grids)
- In digital applications, the UBS logo should be placed in the **top left** of the grid
- In mobile apps with small screen formats (**phones only**, not tablets or desktop), it may sometimes be appropriate to place the logo **top centre**

---

## Clearspace

Ensure sufficient white space between the logo and content for strong brand recognition.

### Minimum clearspace rule

- Leave at minimum **1X clearspace** around a box drawn around the logo
- **X = the height of the letter "B" in the logo**

### Masthead exception

In the masthead of an application only, vertical clearspace can be reduced:
- Still use a minimum of 1X
- But measure from the **top and base of the letters** in the logo, not from the edge of the box surrounding the logo

---

## Favicons

Exceptionally, web applications may use the **Key Symbol alone** as a Favicon:
- Black on a plain background
- No other symbols should be used as Favicons for UBS applications

## Other Logos and Co-branding

- **Creating logos or labels** for applications, products, services and applications is **not permitted**
- The only exceptions are special identities for title sponsorships (not normally used in applications)
- For co-branding requirements, contact the UX Design Strategy Group
