# Brand Naming

> "Simple rule: UBS + descriptive term"

All names created and used by UBS follow established conventions to ensure internal and external consistency around the world.

## General Rules

- Names start with a capital letter, no punctuation or special characters (e.g. no @)
- Use clear and descriptive terms
- Add UBS for definite identification
- Avoid abbreviations wherever possible

## Three Types of Names

### 1. Communication names, abbreviations
E.g. business divisions and departments

### 2. Product, service or application names
E.g. funds, structured products, etc.

### 3. Subsidiary names
Legal names of UBS AG subsidiaries operating under the UBS brand

---

## UBS as a Name

UBS is the name of the organization as a whole and the brand name. UBS is a name in its own right, not an abbreviation. For communication purposes, UBS appears by itself, without a definite article and not as the full legal name.

**For example:**
- UBS is a global financial …
- The staff employed by UBS …

## The UBS Name in Text

Within a sentence, the name UBS appears in the same typeface and color as the rest of the text.

**For example:**
The aim of UBS is to …

### Don't (UBS Name in Text)
- No logo within a text
- No highlighting the name in text
- No part of the logo appears in text
- No article before UBS in German text: UBS is a name in itself

## Communication Names and Abbreviations

### UBS as a Sender
Where UBS is clearly identified as the sender, drop UBS from the name.

### Communication Names Rules
As the logo and sender address are visible, the receiver knows that the material is from UBS. In text, use communication names but never a logo. Third parties mentioning UBS should put UBS in front of the communication name, e.g. UBS Asset Management.

- Avoid abbreviations where possible.
- Indicate full name at least once in text.
- Use a hyphen to add abbreviations to UBS, e.g. UBS-AM.
- If a full word follows UBS, do not add a hyphen but a space in between, e.g. UBS Asset Management.
- Abbreviations need to function globally, e.g. no negative (past and present), humorous or indecent associations.

### Don't (Communication Names)
- Do not use spaces instead of hyphens when adding abbreviations.
- Do not add letters directly to the brand name.
- Do not add other type weights.

## Products, Services and Applications

- Describe content or benefit of the product
- Start name with UBS for legal protection and to hinder imitators
- Apply rules to all product, service, and application names
- Form: "UBS" followed by the most accurate description of the services or benefits
- Use capital letters
- No special characters or punctuation

### Examples
UBS Funds, UBS Saving Account, UBS Customer Card, UBS Portfolio Mortgage, UBS Eurocard, UBS KeyClub*

*UBS KeyClub is an exception. Although not compliant with UBS naming conventions, the name is used because it is so widely recognized and has been established for decades.

Please refer to the downloads section for tips, guides and processes for generating UBS products and services names.

Full naming rules and approval processes are managed by IT, Contracting & Shared Services Legal and Group Brand Experience. Learn more about [naming and name applications](https://ubscloud.sharepoint.com/sites/int-employment-legal-and-corporate-services/SitePages/naming-and-name-applications.aspx) (internal users only).

### Don't (Products & Services)
- Do not create new names that integrate UBS.
- Do not establish individual visual identities (logos/labels) for products, services, and applications.
- Do not create product names that use fancy keywords of a special target group.

## Subsidiaries

### General Rule: UBS + descriptive term + legal form

- Don't use legal entity names in communication. Just talk and write about UBS
- UBS consists of more than 7,500 legal entities. Consider naming from the start when creating new subsidiaries or acquiring other companies
- Naming of joint ventures follows special rules

More information on naming legal entities (internal users only).

Please refer to the downloads section for more info on acquisitions and joint ventures.

### UBS Subsidiaries Rules
- Abbreviate legal form where permitted, e.g. Ltd, Inc., PLC, etc.
- Not more than 30 characters
- No parentheses or periods, where legally possible

### Examples

- **UBS + division + legal form**: e.g. UBS Asset Management Ltd
- **UBS + geographic indicator + legal form**: e.g. UBS France SA
- **UBS + activity business + legal form**: e.g. UBS Securities Inc.
- **Exception: established company names**: e.g. Black & White Investments Ltd (For strategic reasons, UBS can keep well-known company names of new acquisitions for a limited time.)

### Endorsed or Separate Subsidiaries

- **Endorsed**: e.g. Wolfsberg UBS Center for Education and Dialogue
- **Separate**: e.g. O'Connor LLC
