# UBS Look and Feel

> Source: UBS Frontify Portal, Standard Basics > Design Basics & Brand > Look and Feel

## Three Pillars

### 1. Bright
**Sparkling clean.** A bright white, seamless backdrop sets the stage. On screen, in print, or wherever the brand appears.

- White, light and bright
- Black for clarity
- Accent in UBS red and secondary colours to enhance the corporate colours

### 2. Message
**Light and smart.** Two components:
1. A **keyline** that hooks the reader
2. An **infoline** that's clear and convincing

### 3. Pictures
**Bright moments.** Pictures are bright and light. And human. They tell the story of clients, their active lifestyle and purposeful mindset.

## Logo
**Strong.** Known all around the world, clearly confident. Black and red. The semibold logo is standard.
