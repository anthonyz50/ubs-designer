# Brand Architecture

> "We are one UBS: one firm, one brand, one logo."

## UBS Masterbrand

We operate as a single brand – building recognition, brand equity and a strong positioning through everything we do.

This means all business divisions, group functions and UBS subsidiaries use the UBS logo. By default, all internal and external products and services of UBS entities are offered in the name of UBS.

### Key Rules

- The UBS logo identifies UBS as a whole
- It embodies our values and heritage
- **No other logos, brands or sub-brands are permitted.**
- Exceptions are granted by Group Brand Experience only for a very small number of special initiatives.

To ensure that all UBS touchpoints are on-brand, the UBS Branding and Naming Guidelines define rules for the correct use of the UBS Masterbrand throughout all possible applications. By adhering to these guidelines and strictly following the related rules, you contribute to strengthen the UBS brand and generate recognition.

**Reference:** [UBS Branding and Naming Guidelines](https://bw.policies.ubs.com/policies/1/9/1/17/1/1-G-002864.html) (internal users only)

## Exceptional Cases

Specific criteria need to be fulfilled for these exceptional branding options:

### Endorsed Brands

In rare cases, an entity, product or service might require its own logo brand appearance. Evaluating criteria will include: reputational risk to the master brand, new target audience or market, non-core business, regulatory requirement etc.

**Examples:** (images showing endorsed brand examples)

### Joint Venture Brands

For joint business initiatives, where another one or more brands/entities are involved with UBS, the appropriate brand option requires case-by-case evaluations.

Evaluating criteria for the relevant branding option will include: market presence or (brand) strength, relevance for clients, long-term business strategy, management control, reputational risk, regulatory requirements, etc.

**Examples:** (images showing joint venture brand examples)

### Co-brands Using a Composite Logo

Composite logo branding is limited to strategic marketing or partnerships with another brand (e.g. sponsorship), strengthening the reputation of both brands, equally.

These are evaluated by Group Brand Experience and Sponsoring.

**Example:** (image showing co-brand composite logo example)

### Separate Brands

The cases for a separate brand may only be permitted for acquisitions before migration to UBS, as part of a transitional period. Or for a non-core business.

These are evaluated on a case-by-case basis, granted with conditional requirements.
