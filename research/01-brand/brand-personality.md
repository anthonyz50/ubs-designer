# Brand Personality

> In UBS we operate as one, single brand with one logo, one identity and one personality in our brand framework.

Explore our brand and discover what makes us different. This is how our clients should perceive UBS.

## Brand Personality Traits

### 1. Positive
We are optimistic by nature

### 2. Forward looking
We are contemporary with purposeful intelligence

### 3. Empowering
We inspire connections in the hands of our clients

### 4. Warm and human
We are always listening to clients' unique needs to realize their ambitions
